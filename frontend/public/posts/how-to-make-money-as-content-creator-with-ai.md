## Step 1: Pick a Niche That Pays

Not all niches monetise equally. The highest-earning content categories in 2026:

**Finance and investing** — The most lucrative niche. Brand deals pay $500–$5,000 per post. Affiliate commissions from fintech products range from $50–$200 per signup. Finance creators with 50k followers regularly earn $5,000+/month.

**Business and entrepreneurship** — Close second. Digital product sales (courses, templates) convert extremely well. A single $97 Notion template can sell hundreds of copies from one viral video.

**Health and nutrition** — Massive audience, strong supplement affiliate market, and recurring purchases make this highly monetisable.

**AI tools and productivity** — The fastest-growing niche in 2026. Early creators are building large audiences quickly because demand outpaces supply.

**Career development** — Strong in emerging markets. Resume templates, interview prep courses, and LinkedIn guides sell well to ambitious professionals.

## Step 2: Create Content That Converts

The goal isn't views. Views are vanity. The goal is followers, then buyers.

**Three content types that build a monetisable audience:**

*Educational content* — "How to do X" videos attract people actively looking to solve a problem. These viewers are more likely to buy because they're already in solution-seeking mode.

*Myth-busting content* — "The truth about X" gets shares because people tag friends they disagree with. Shares grow your audience faster than views alone.

*Results content* — "I did X for 30 days and here's what happened" builds credibility and trust. Trust converts to sales.

## Step 4: Turn Followers Into Income

Once you have a growing audience, monetisation happens in layers.

**Layer 1 — Platform income ($0–$500/month at 10k followers)**

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels all pay directly based on views. It's not life-changing at small scale but it compounds as you grow.

**Layer 2 — Affiliate marketing ($200–$2,000/month)**

Sign up for affiliate programmes related to your niche. Finance creators use Cowrywise, Risevest, or Bamboo affiliate programmes. Tech creators use software affiliate programmes paying 20–40% recurring commissions.

The key: only recommend tools you actually use. Your audience can tell the difference.

**Layer 3 — Digital products ($500–$5,000/month)**

Create something once, sell it forever. A 30-minute video course on Gumroad or Selar, a template pack, a guide — anything your audience would pay $15–$97 for.

The content creation niche itself converts well: "How I make videos with AI" as a course sells to everyone who watches your content and wonders how you do it.

**Layer 4 — Brand deals ($1,000–$10,000/month at 50k+ followers)**

Brands approach you, or you approach them with a media kit. At 50k engaged followers in a commercial niche, you can charge $500–$2,000 per sponsored post.

## Step 5: Optimise for SEO and Discovery

The creators who compound their growth understand that SEO matters as much for video as for websites.

**On TikTok and Reels:** Use the full caption. Front-load your target keyword. "How to invest ₦5,000" in the caption helps TikTok's algorithm match your video to people searching that topic.

**On YouTube Shorts:** Your title is searchable. Optimise it like a blog post title. "3 investing mistakes beginners make in Nigeria" ranks for that exact search query.

**On your website or blog:** Create written content about your video topics. A blog post titled "How to make money as a content creator" gets Google traffic that discovers your video content.
