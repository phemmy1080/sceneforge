# The YouTube Shorts Algorithm in 2026: What Actually Drives Views

YouTube Shorts crossed 70 billion daily views in 2024. The platform is actively pushing Shorts to compete with TikTok — which means creators who understand the algorithm right now have a massive opportunity.

Here's what actually drives views on YouTube Shorts in 2026.

## The Two Phases of Shorts Distribution

Every Shorts video goes through two phases:

**Phase 1 — The test audience.** YouTube shows your video to a small initial batch of viewers, typically people already subscribed to you or viewers with similar watch histories. The algorithm watches how they respond.

**Phase 2 — Broader distribution.** If your Phase 1 metrics are strong, YouTube pushes the video to a broader audience through the Shorts feed. This is where viral growth happens.

Most creators never make it past Phase 1. Understanding why is the key to growing.

## The Metrics That Matter Most

### 1. View completion rate
The most important signal. YouTube measures what percentage of viewers watch your entire Short. Industry data suggests videos with 70%+ completion rates get pushed heavily in Phase 2.

This is why hooks are so critical. If your first 3 seconds don't hold attention, your completion rate tanks, your Phase 1 metrics fail, and YouTube stops showing the video.

### 2. Likes to views ratio
A strong like rate (3%+) signals to YouTube that the content is high quality and resonates with viewers. Encourage likes naturally — "like if this helped" at the end of a useful video converts well.

### 3. Comments
Comments signal deep engagement. YouTube's algorithm treats comments as a strong positive signal — they mean viewers were moved enough to say something. Content that asks questions or has a debatable angle generates more comments.

### 4. Subscriber conversion
YouTube tracks whether new viewers subscribe after watching your Short. High subscription rates signal that your content has strong "follow for more" appeal — exactly what YouTube wants to promote.

## What the Algorithm Doesn't Care About

**Posting time** — Unlike TikTok, YouTube Shorts doesn't have a strong posting-time effect. The Shorts feed is personalised, not chronological.

**Video length** — Shorter isn't always better. A 55-second Short with 72% completion beats a 15-second Short with 40% completion.

**Tags** — Tags have minimal effect on Shorts discoverability. Focus on the title and description for keyword signals.

## How to Optimise for Phase 2 Distribution

**Open with a pattern interrupt.** Start with something surprising, counterintuitive, or emotionally charged. "You're brushing your teeth wrong" outperforms "Here's how to brush your teeth" every time.

**Remove every second of dead air.** Jump cuts every 5-8 seconds. No pauses. No filler words. Viewers who see any hesitation scroll immediately.

**Make the payoff worth the watch.** The promise in your hook must be delivered clearly before the video ends. Viewers who feel cheated leave without engaging.

**End with a reason to subscribe.** "I post daily tips on X" works better than "Subscribe for more." Give them a specific reason.
