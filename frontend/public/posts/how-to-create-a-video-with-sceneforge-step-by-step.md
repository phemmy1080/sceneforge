# How to Create a Video with SceneForge: A Complete Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

SceneForge turns any idea into a finished, ready-to-publish video in under 60 seconds — no camera, no editing software, no experience required. This guide walks you through every step with screenshots so you know exactly what to expect.

By the end of this guide you will have a complete video with AI voiceover, stock footage or AI-generated visuals, subtitles, and background music — ready to upload to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Instagram Reels.

**What you need:** A SceneForge account (free at sceneraforge.com). Nothing else.

---

## Step 1 — Create a New Project

::step-image::step1-setup

When you log in to SceneForge, you land on the Projects screen. Click **New project** in the top left corner of the sidebar — or the large New project button in the centre of the screen if you have no projects yet.

This opens the **Setup** page — the starting point for every video.

**What to do:**
- Give your project a name so you can find it later
- You'll configure everything on the next screen

---

## Step 2 — Configure Your Project

::step-image::step2-config

The Setup screen has five key settings. Fill them in from top to bottom:

**Content niche** — Choose the category that best describes your content. Options include Finance, Fitness, Tech, Lifestyle, Business, Education, and more. SceneForge uses your niche to tune the AI — Finance scripts sound different from Fitness scripts.

**Topic hints** — Optional but powerful. Type in specific angles, keywords, or talking points you want the AI to include. Press Enter after each one. If you leave this blank, the AI generates ideas entirely from your niche and style settings.

**Video style** — Choose how you want the video structured. Viral/Hook-first works best for TikTok. Educational works well for YouTube. Storytelling builds emotional connection.

**Platform** — Select where this video will be published. This sets the aspect ratio and target duration automatically.

**Tone and audience** — Describe who you are talking to. "Beginner investors aged 25-35" gives the AI a specific lens to write through.

Click **Generate ideas →** when you are ready.

---

## Step 3 — Choose Your Best Idea

::step-image::step3-ideas

SceneForge generates **six viral content ideas** based on your niche, style, and topic hints. Each idea includes:

- A **title** — the angle for the video
- A **hook** — the opening line that stops the scroll
- An **angle** — the strategic lens the content takes

Read through all six. The **SceneForge Co-pilot** — the floating assistant in the bottom right corner — is particularly useful here. It has already read all six ideas and knows your niche and platform. Click it and ask:

- "Which idea will perform best on TikTok for Finance content?"
- "Compare all the ideas and score them out of 10"
- "Which hook is the strongest?"

The co-pilot analyses every idea individually and gives you a specific recommendation with reasoning. It will not mix up the ideas or give generic advice — it knows exactly what you are working with.

Click on the idea you want to develop. It highlights in purple. Then click **Write script →**.

**Tip:** The first idea is usually the most conventional. Ideas 3-5 are often the most interesting angles.

---

## Step 4 — Review and Edit Your Script

::step-image::step4-script

SceneForge writes your full voiceover script based on the idea you selected. The script streams live — you will see it appear word by word, the same way ChatGPT responds.

The script is structured for short-form video:
- A strong hook in the first sentence
- Clear, short sentences designed for voiceover
- A call to action at the end

**You can edit anything.** Click anywhere in the script to make changes. Common edits:
- Personalise it with your own examples or local references
- Adjust the tone if it feels too formal or too casual
- Add specific statistics or facts you know are accurate

When you are happy with the script, click **Continue to scenes →**.

**Co-pilot tip at this step:** The co-pilot knows your full script. Click it and ask:
- "Improve the hook to be more attention-grabbing"
- "Shorten this script by 20 percent"
- "Make it more emotional and personal"
- "Write a stronger call to action"

It rewrites the exact section you need — you do not have to describe what the script says. It already knows.

---

## Step 5 — Edit Your Scenes

::step-image::step5-scenes

SceneForge automatically splits your script into scenes — each one is a separate segment of your video with its own visual.

Each scene card shows:
- The **text** that will be spoken in that segment
- The **duration** in seconds
- A **visual prompt** — the description used to find matching footage

**What to edit:**
- **Duration** — drag to adjust how long each scene plays. For TikTok, keep scenes between 5-10 seconds.
- **Visual prompt** — make it more specific for better footage matches. "Nigerian entrepreneur in modern Lagos office" works better than "business person".
- **Text** — edit individual scene text without changing the whole script.

You can also add new scenes with the + button, or delete scenes you do not need.

Click **Voice & visuals →** when your scenes look right.

**Co-pilot tip at this step:** Ask the co-pilot to review your scene structure before moving on:
- "Are any of my scenes too long for TikTok?"
- "Suggest better visual prompts for my Finance content"
- "Is my scene order good for viewer retention?"

It checks your scene durations against platform best practices and flags anything that might hurt watch time.

---

## Step 6 — Choose Voice and Visuals

::step-image::step6-voice

This is where your video gets its look and sound. The screen has four sections:

**Neural voice** — Choose from 24+ voices. Click any voice to hear a sample. For Finance and Business content, calm authoritative voices (Marcus, David) perform best. For Fitness and Energy content, faster-paced voices work better.

**Visual source** — Four options:
- *Mixed* — Pexels stock footage plus AI fill-in where needed (recommended for most)
- *Stock video* — Pexels video only
- *Stock photos* — Pexels photos only
- *AI images (DALL-E 3)* — Pro and Studio plans only. Generates a custom AI image for every scene.

**Cinematic motion** — Choose how the camera moves. Auto cycles through Ken Burns zoom and pan effects per scene. This makes stock footage feel produced and dynamic.

**Scene transitions** — How scenes connect. Crossfade is the most cinematic. Blur fade gives a modern feel. Cut is the fastest to render.

**Background music** — Choose a mood or select None. The music is mixed at low volume so the voiceover always stays clear.

Click **Render video →** when everything is configured.

**Co-pilot tip at this step:** Not sure which voice or music to pick? Ask the co-pilot:
- "Which voice works best for Finance content on TikTok?"
- "Should I use Ken Burns zoom or pan for this type of video?"
- "What music mood fits my niche?"

It gives you a specific recommendation based on your niche, platform, and video tone — not generic advice.

---

## Step 7 — Render and Download Your Video

::step-image::step7-export

SceneForge now produces your video. A progress bar shows each stage:

1. Synthesising voice (the AI generates your voiceover)
2. Fetching visuals (stock footage or AI images matched to each scene)
3. Compositing scenes (combining video, audio, and subtitles)
4. Adding transitions and music
5. Final export

Rendering typically takes **60-120 seconds** depending on the number of scenes and visual source.

When complete, you will see your finished video with download options:

- **Download MP4** — the complete video ready to upload anywhere
- **Download CapCut bundle** — individual scene files plus a draft file for direct import into CapCut
- **Scene bundle** — individual MP4 files for each scene

---

## Step 8 — Share Your Video

::step-image::step8-share

After downloading, the Share panel appears automatically. Select your target platform — TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or LinkedIn — and SceneForge generates:

- A platform-optimised **caption** in the right tone for that platform
- **6-7 hashtags** relevant to your niche and content
- The **best posting times** based on your niche and platform
- A one-line **posting tip** based on that platform's algorithm

Click **Copy caption**, **Copy hashtags**, or **Copy all** to grab everything at once.

Then click **Open TikTok upload** (or whichever platform you chose) to go directly to that platform's upload page.

**Co-pilot tip at this step:** Ask the co-pilot for posting advice specific to your video:
- "When is the best time to post Finance content on TikTok in Nigeria?"
- "Write a better caption for this video"
- "What hashtags should I use for this niche?"

It knows your niche, platform, and content — the advice is specific to your video, not generic. Your video is already downloaded — drag it in, paste your caption, and post.

## The Co-pilot: Your AI Assistant at Every Step

::step-image::step-copilot

One feature that makes SceneForge different from other AI video tools is the **Co-pilot** — a floating AI assistant that stays with you throughout the entire workflow.

Unlike a generic chatbot, the Co-pilot knows exactly where you are in the process:

- **On the Ideas step** — it has read all six generated ideas and can compare, score, and recommend the best one for your specific niche and platform
- **On the Script step** — it knows your full script and can improve the hook, shorten it, add emotion, or write a stronger CTA on demand
- **On the Scene editor** — it reviews your scene durations and visual prompts, flags anything that might hurt retention, and suggests improvements
- **On Voice & Visuals** — it recommends the best voice, music, and motion settings for your niche
- **On Export** — it suggests the best posting times and can generate a platform-specific caption and hashtags

You can also type any question at any time — the co-pilot is not limited to the quick action buttons. Ask it anything about your video, your niche, or how SceneForge works and it will respond with context-aware advice.

The co-pilot pulse animation lets you know it is active and ready. Click the floating button in the bottom right corner of any screen to open it.
